Privacy Policy

Meri EMI Kitab – EMI Tracker · by AppsByNeel

Effective date: 29 October 2026

In short: Meri EMI Kitab works completely offline. Everything you enter stays on your phone. We do not collect, store, share or sell any of your data, and the app has no ads, analytics or accounts.

This policy explains how the Meri EMI Kitab Android app ("the app", "we") handles your information. It applies only to this app, published on Google Play by AppsByNeel under the package name com.appsbyneel.emikitab.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive data. The app does not even request the internet permission, so it is technically unable to send your information to us or to anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage that other apps cannot read:

  • EMI and loan details you enter: names, categories, amounts, dates, number of installments, interest rate, lender name and notes.
  • Payment status of each installment (paid or upcoming) and paid dates.
  • Photos you choose to attach, such as a bill or loan document.
  • Your preferences: the name used in the Home greeting, language, theme and reminder settings.

This information is never uploaded. Cloud backup is turned off for the app, so your records are not copied to Google Drive or other backups.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications – to show reminders before an EMI is due. Reminders are created on your phone; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in Android settings at any time.
  • Camera and photos – the app requests no camera or storage permission. If you attach a photo, Android's own camera or photo picker is used, and the app only receives the single picture you choose.

4. Sharing of information

We do not share, sell or transfer your information to any third party. The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.

Two features open other apps, but only when you tap them:

  • Share app opens Android's share menu with a message and a Google Play link. Nothing about your EMIs is included.
  • Give feedback opens your email app with a message addressed to us. It is pre-filled with the app version, Android version and phone model to help us fix problems; you can edit or remove this before sending. If you send it, we use your email only to reply to you and do not share it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can remove it at any time:

  • delete individual EMIs inside the app,
  • use Settings → Delete all local data, or
  • uninstall the app, which permanently removes all of its data.

Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side.

6. Security

Your records are kept in the app's private storage on your phone, protected by Android's app sandbox and your phone's screen lock. Because nothing is sent over the internet, your data is not exposed to online breaches. Please keep your phone secure, since anyone who can unlock it can open the app.

7. Children's privacy

The app is meant for adults managing their own loans and payments. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released.
